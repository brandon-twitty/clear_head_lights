The Hazard:
Dangerous Dimming

Over time, Missouri's extreme summer heat and winter road salt accelerate oxidation on modern acrylic headlight lenses. This "fogging" isn't just cosmetic—it can block up to 80% of your headlight's light output.

This severe light loss creates a major safety hazard on dark rural roads, substantially increasing the risk of deer strikes and low-visibility collisions.

Warning: Typical DIY wipe-on kits only mask oxidation for 1 to 3 months. Our industrial-grade restoration is deep-cleansing and built to last.

Our Proprietary 10-Step Process

We don’t believe in shortcut fixes or simple "wipe-away" solutions. We use a professional-grade, multi-stage sequence to fully restore clarity and seal the lens against future damage.

1

Surface Decontamination

Initial surface preparation using a specialized vinegar spray and clean wipe.

2

Precision Masking

Protecting your vehicle’s surrounding paint with high-quality blue painter's tape.

3

Deep Chemical Clean

Application of Mothers Mag wheel cleaner to begin loosening surface oxidation.

4

Active Dwell Time

Allowing chemical cleaners to dwell for 10 minutes to draw out deep-set yellowing.

5

Microfiber Extraction

Wiping away all emulsified yellowing and microscopic paint oxidation residue.

6

UV Protection

Liberal application of high-grade Carnauba Wax to establish a protective base barrier.

7

Protective Dwell

Allowing 10 minutes for the protection layer to set and lock out moisture and oxygen.

8

Final Wipe-Down

Clearing off setting residues to prep the newly sealed acrylic surface.

9

Mechanical Buffing

Polishing for 5 minutes per light with an electric buffer and pristine pads to clarify.

10

Site Cleanup

Removing protective masking tape, buffing off margins, and performing a final safety review.
